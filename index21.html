Common questions

Short answers to what most applicants ask first.

How does the system pick schemes for me?

It compares your profile with configured scheme rules such as age, gender, category, sector, business stage, location, project cost, household income and education.

Is a high match a guarantee?

No. The match percentage is only a prototype recommendation score. Final eligibility is decided by the relevant department or lender.

What is the acceptance rate?

Acceptance rate is calculated as approved applications divided by applications received. Prototype/demo figures should be replaced with verified official scheme-level statistics.

Can I calculate EMI?

Yes. Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure in the EMI calculator.

Do I need to sign in?

Browsing schemes does not require an account. Signing in allows this prototype to associate your recently visited schemes with your local account.
